The Minnesota Initiative

Where the Work Stands

A developed foundation entering its next stage.

The clinical and membership foundations have been developed and tested in Minnesota over more than fifteen years. The complete cooperative has not yet been built at scale.

The Minnesota Initiative exists to cross that distance deliberately.

Developed

  • The PrimaryCare.Solutions clinical framework
  • The arc-and-hub operating logic
  • Principles for integration and validation
  • The relationship between capacity, agency, and participation
  • Foundational membership experience
  • The six-stage development sequence

Now in Development

  • Representative clinical walkthroughs
  • The outcome and measurement structure
  • Minnesota cost and access analysis
  • Financing comparisons
  • Institutional discovery
  • Cooperative coalition formation

Not Yet Claimed

  • A fully operating cooperative
  • Population-scale clinical or financial results
  • A finalized founding population
  • A complete capitalization plan
  • Final governance and regulatory structures

Clear boundaries matter. This initiative will distinguish what has been established, what the evidence reasonably supports, and what must still be demonstrated.

The objective is not to move quickly enough to appear active. It is to complete the work required to build responsibly.

Current position last reviewed: September 2026.
