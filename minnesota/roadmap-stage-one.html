The Minnesota Roadmap
Stage One

Establish the Clinical Build

What must the system be capable of delivering across a complete arc of care?

The Work

At least ten representative patient needs will be walked through the complete PrimaryCare.Solutions framework.

Each walkthrough will define:

  • the role of the primary care hub;
  • the stages of the patient's care;
  • the clinical and therapeutic tools that may be required;
  • the sequence and coordination of those tools;
  • expected outcomes and decision points;
  • escalation and referral criteria;
  • the transition toward self-management;
  • and the resources required across the complete arc.

The Required Result

A pressure-tested clinical operating map identifying the people, time, tools, facilities, relationships, and decisions required to deliver the model.

The Readiness Standard

The system can describe what it would deliver, how progress would be evaluated, and what resources would be required across a representative range of patient needs.
