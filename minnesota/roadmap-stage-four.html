The Minnesota Roadmap
Stage Four

Establish Anchor Participation

Is there a sufficient founding population to support a responsible first build?

The Work

The initiative will identify employers, cooperatives, unions, community organizations, public entities, and other membership groups with a meaningful need for more effective primary care.

Early conversations will examine:

  • population needs;
  • current healthcare spending;
  • access problems;
  • preventable escalation;
  • geographic concentration;
  • willingness to participate;
  • and the conditions under which an organization could become an anchor member or purchaser.

The Required Result

A defined founding population, credible participation commitments, and enough concentrated demand to support the first operating model.

The Readiness Standard

The initiative has a population large and coherent enough to support the clinical, financial, and operational requirements established in the earlier stages.
