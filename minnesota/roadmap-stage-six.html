The Minnesota Roadmap
Stage Six

Expand Without Dilution

How can the model grow without losing clinical coherence or local accountability?

The Work

The initiative will determine which elements must remain consistent across participating clinics and which should remain locally controlled.

Shared functions may include:

  • clinical and operating standards;
  • training;
  • outcome measurement;
  • purchasing;
  • technology;
  • legal and administrative support;
  • financial safeguards;
  • and cooperative development resources.

Local clinics must retain the ability to respond to the needs, relationships, and resources of their own communities.

The Required Result

A replication and federation model that protects common purpose while allowing local ownership and adaptation.

The Readiness Standard

Additional communities can use the shared framework and infrastructure without weakening care quality, member accountability, or local decision-making.
