The Minnesota Roadmap
Stage Three

Establish the Fee Strategy

How should the system be financed so that payment supports the clinical model?

The Work

The initiative will compare financial structures capable of sustaining continuity while protecting members from unreasonable risk.

The analysis may include:

  • individual and household membership;
  • employer or organizational contributions;
  • pooled primary care funding;
  • defined cost-sharing;
  • mutual or cooperative risk arrangements;
  • and coordination with major-medical insurance.

Each option will be tested against the clinical and resource requirements established in the earlier stages.

The Required Result

A financing architecture defining contributions, covered primary care resources, member responsibility, reserves, insurance boundaries, and the conditions required for viability.

The Readiness Standard

The proposed structure can finance the clinical model without rewarding unnecessary volume, restricting appropriate care, or transferring unreasonable risk to patients or the cooperative.
