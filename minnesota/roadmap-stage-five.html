The Minnesota Roadmap
Stage Five

Hold the Arc

What infrastructure is required to preserve coordination, accountability, and purpose in operation?

The Work

The initiative will design the operating and governance structures required to hold the model together.

This includes:

  • the responsibilities of the primary care hub;
  • clinical decision authority;
  • member participation and governance;
  • practitioner standards;
  • data and outcome review;
  • referral and escalation pathways;
  • quality safeguards;
  • financial oversight;
  • education and training;
  • and shared administrative infrastructure.

The Required Result

A complete operating and governance design capable of holding the patient's arc, the cooperative's purpose, and the institution's accountability.

The Readiness Standard

Clinical judgment, member ownership, operational responsibility, and financial oversight are clearly assigned and able to function together.
